## Refinery 0.3.49

- Settings now has separate Recording and Apps pages, with iCloud sync in General and less space above page content.
- History is easier to scan: open a result to see and copy the full text or its original transcription, and confirm before clearing it.
- Screen context is captured automatically when recording starts if its toggle is enabled. The separate shortcut and menu command have been removed.
- The writing service picker uses a lightning icon for OpenAI and Claude, while Apple Intelligence uses its own neutral symbol.

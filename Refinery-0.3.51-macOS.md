## Refinery 0.3.51

- The unused timer item is hidden from the menu bar when you are not recording, and Refinery removes both status items as it quits. This prevents an extra item lingering around an update or relaunch.
- The sound Preview button now sits before its switch in Settings.
- iCloud's production schema now supports named writing styles. Sync errors are shown in plain language; local writing settings remain saved if iCloud is unavailable.
